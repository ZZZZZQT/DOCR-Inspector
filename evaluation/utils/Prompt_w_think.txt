## Error Type Definitions

1.  **Text misrecognized as formula**: Plain text or paragraphs are incorrectly classified or rendered as mathematical/chemical formulae.
2.  **Text misrecognized as table**: Ordinary text or inline content is incorrectly labeled as a table structure.
3.  **Text misrecognized as table**: Plain text or other text like caption is incorrectly labeled as a title structure.
4.  **Displayed formula misrecognized as text**: A displayed formula is recognized as plain continuous text, flattening or losing its mathematical structure.
5.  **Title format recognition error**: The heading's level, style, or format is misrecognized (e.g., wrong level assignment, incorrect/missing separators).
6.  **List format recognition error**: The structure of ordered/unordered lists (markers, hierarchy, indentation) is incorrectly parsed or lost.
7.  **Superscript citation format in text recognition error**: Superscripts/subscripts or footnote markers are recognized as baseline characters or are misplaced/lost.
8.  **Text paragraph format error**: Paragraph boundaries, line breaks, or indentation are misrecognized, causing redundant breaks or merges.
9.  **Text segment lost**: Entire segments or fragments present in the image are missing from the OCR output.
10.  **Text redundancy**: The OCR output contains extra text not present in the image.
11. **Text repetition**: The same passage, sentence, or character appears multiple times in the OCR output.
12. **Text characters lost**: Single or a small number of characters are not recognized or are omitted entirely (e.g., truncated words).
13. **Text punctuation recognition error**: Punctuation is omitted, misrecognized, or misplaced, altering sentence segmentation/semantics.
14. **Extra/missing spaces in text**: Spaces are incorrectly inserted (splitting words) or omitted (concatenating words).
15. **Text character recognition error**: Individual characters are misrecognized as visually similar alternatives or misread.
16. **Inline formula missed detection**: Inline mathematical/chemical fragments are not detected as formulas but treated as ordinary continuous text/whitespace.
17. **Inline formula representation style error**: Equivalent or similar inline formulas are rendered in multiple inconsistent styles within the document.
18. **Inline formula recognition error**: Inline formulas are misrecognized, including structural loss, character errors, or syntactic corruption, changing the mathematical meaning.
19. **Displayed formula character recognition error**: Individual characters or symbols inside a displayed formula are misrecognized, substituted, or omitted.
20. **Displayed formula structure recognition error**: The two-dimensional layout of a displayed formula is misrecognized, and hierarchical/aligned relationships are lost or incorrectly assembled.
21. **Displayed formula syntax error**: The OCR output formula has syntactic problems (e.g., unclosed parentheses, truncated commands) that make it unparsable.
22. **Partial displayed formula missing**: Some subformulas, subexpressions, or critical symbols are entirely omitted during OCR.
23. **Table recognition corruption**: A severe failure in table recognition where the overall table grammar is corrupted, causing a completely malformed table structure.
24. **Partial table redundancy**: The model erroneously includes surrounding or adjacent text as part of the table or places it inside cells.
25. **Missing table row/column**: One or more complete rows or columns of a table are missing in the OCR output.
26. **Table merged cell error**: Errors in detecting or reconstructing merged cells (incorrectly merging cells or failing to detect a merged cell).
27. **Table cell recognition lost**: One or more table cell positions are recognized as empty or are entirely missing from the output.
28. **Table cell content recognition error**: Contents inside a table cell are misrecognized or partially lost (character mistakes, number errors, or loss of internal structure).
